Empowering the residents of Madhya Pradesh with seamless single-window digital access to citizen services.

MP Suvidha is an independent digital service information and application demonstration portal designed to help citizens discover and access information about essential services in Madhya Pradesh.

The portal showcases a unified citizen experience: single-window discovery across 12 public service categories, paperless document upload simulation, transparent multi-stage application tracking, and verifiable demonstration certificates.

Important Independent Disclaimer: MP Suvidha is an independent demonstration portal. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or an official website of the Government of Madhya Pradesh or any of its departments. All demonstration certificates and application workflows provided herein are simulated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MP Suvidha an official government website?

No. MP Suvidha is an independent informational and demonstration portal that helps citizens discover public services, understand eligibility, and test digital workflows in Madhya Pradesh.

What file types and size limits are supported for document uploads?

You can upload files in PDF, JPG, JPEG, and PNG formats up to 5 MB per document.

How can I track my submitted application?

Simply navigate to the Track Application section, enter your Application ID (e.g., MPS-2026-08142) along with your registered contact number or email, and inspect live stage progress.

Are fees charged for demonstration applications?

No real payments are collected. All fees shown on service cards represent standard informational demonstration estimates.

How does digital certificate verification work?

Each certificate is issued with a unique tamper-evident certificate number (e.g., MPS-CERT-2026-09831) and QR code, which can be verified in real time on the Verify Certificate page.

Privacy Policy & Information

MP Suvidha is an independent demonstration portal. All data submitted is stored strictly within a local demonstration SQLite database. Passwords are encrypted using standard PBKDF2 hashing.

No real government biometric servers, DigiLocker databases, or official records are connected. Please do not submit confidential financial or biometric credentials.

Terms & Conditions

This portal is provided strictly for educational and demonstration purposes. Certificates generated by this system are simulated demonstration documents and hold no statutory or legal authority with the Government of Madhya Pradesh or any official agency.
