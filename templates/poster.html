🏛 Project Vision & Purpose
Transforming Public Service Delivery

JanSeva Connect is a centralized, digital e-governance platform providing transparent access to citizen services. Inspired by Madhya Pradesh's MP e-Services (services.mp.gov.in), it replaces physical paperwork with automated online workflows, real-time tracking, digital certificate issuance, and an academic evaluation system.

⚡ System Architecture & Technology
Clean, Zero-Build Stack

Engineered without heavy Node build tools for maximum portability, security, and lightning execution on any machine.
